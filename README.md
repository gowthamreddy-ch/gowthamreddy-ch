## Recent work · October 2026

- **Published the portfolio and two application repositories**, bringing demos, setup instructions, and engineering decisions into one place.
- **Compared three code-review approaches in ReviewLens.** The ten-case synthetic development comparison preserves false positives and unsupported reasoning, alongside successful findings. [Read the results](https://gowthamreddy-ch.github.io/assets/reviewlens/results.txt).
- **Evaluated personalization against popularity in ReelLab.** The recorded MovieLens 100K test showed no clear overall NDCG@10 advantage; the three-rating experiment exposed a weaker cold-profile result. [Explore the evaluation](https://gowthamreddy-ch.github.io/projects/reellab/#evidence-title).

**Next research direction:** better abstention when evidence is missing, independent review of model findings, and evaluation on reserved cases after the approach is frozen.
